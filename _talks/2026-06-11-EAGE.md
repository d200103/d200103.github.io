[1] C. Ding and R. Lin, “An interpolated tensor-wheel completion algorithm for a generalized five-dimensional arbitrary regular- or irregular-grid seismic data reconstruction,” in 87th EAGE Annual Conference & Exhibition, vol. 2026, no. 1. European Association of Geoscientists & Engineers, 2026, pp. 1–5.

**报告名：** An interpolated tensor-wheel completion algorithm for a generalized five-dimensional arbitrary regular- or irregular-grid seismic data reconstruction

**摘要：** Multidimensional seismic data reconstruction has attracted considerable attention in recent years. Low-rank tensor decomposition has been widely used as an effective approximation framework for five-dimensional (5D) seismic data reconstruction. However, many existing tensor-based methods suffer from decomposition imbalance and an insufficient ability to fully exploit correlations across different tensor dimensions. In this study, we propose an interpolated tensor wheel completion (I-TWC) algorithm for the simultaneous reconstruction and denoising of 5D seismic data. A generalized interpolator operator incorporating the true spatial coordinates of seismic acquisition is introduced, enabling I-TWC to handle both regular and irregular sampling grids. The seismic data is decomposed into a set of ring factors and a core factor with a wheel topology through multilinear products, which allows the method to more effectively capture inter-dimensional correlations while maintaining a balanced low-rank decomposition. Compared with conventional matrix-based and tensor-based low-rank reconstruction approaches, synthetic and field data examples demonstrate that the proposed I-TWC method achieves higher reconstruction accuracy. In addition, the reconstructed seismic traces exhibit improved continuity and curvature preservation, indicating their effectiveness for complex multidimensional seismic data recovery.



[2] C. Ding, R. Lin, and S. Yu, “Simultaneous reconstruction and denoising via a self-supervised low-rank framework deeptensor,” in 87th EAGE Annual Conference & Exhibition, vol. 2026, no. 1. European Association of Geoscientists & Engineers, 2026, pp. 1–5.


**报告名：** Simultaneous reconstruction and denoising via a self-supervised low-rank framework deeptensor

**摘要：** Seismic data are often affected by missing traces and noise. While conventional low-rank approximation methods often result in over-smoothing or artifacts when facing non-Gaussian and coherent noise, supervised deep learning based methods are constrained by the scarcity of clean training labels and limited generalization capabilities. To handle this challenges, we propose a self-supervised low-rank framework, termed DeepTensor, for the simultaneous reconstruction and denoising of seismic data. Inspired by deep image priors, we use deep neural networks to parameterize the factors of a low-rank decomposition in a zero-shot manner, thereby integrating the implicit regularization of network architectures with the intrinsic low-rank structure of multidimensional seismic data. Numerical examples demonstrate that the proposed DeepTensor method achieves effective reconstruction and denoising performance and can be extended to higher dimensions seismic process.
