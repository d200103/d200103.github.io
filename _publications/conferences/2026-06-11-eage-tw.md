**Title:** An interpolated tensor-wheel completion algorithm for a generalized five-dimensional arbitrary regular- or irregular-grid seismic data reconstruction

**Authors:** Chengxiong Ding and Rongzhi Lin

**Conference:** 87th EAGE Annual Conference & Exhibition

**Date:** June 11, 2026

**Abstract:** Multidimensional seismic data reconstruction has attracted considerable attention in recent years. Low-rank tensor decomposition has been widely used as an effective approximation framework for five-dimensional (5D) seismic data reconstruction. However, many existing tensor-based methods suffer from decomposition imbalance and an insufficient ability to fully exploit correlations across different tensor dimensions. In this study, we propose an interpolated tensor wheel completion (I-TWC) algorithm for the simultaneous reconstruction and denoising of 5D seismic data. A generalized interpolator operator incorporating the true spatial coordinates of seismic acquisition is introduced, enabling I-TWC to handle both regular and irregular sampling grids. The seismic data is decomposed into a set of ring factors and a core factor with a wheel topology through multilinear products, which allows the method to more effectively capture inter-dimensional correlations while maintaining a balanced low-rank decomposition. Compared with conventional matrix-based and tensor-based low-rank reconstruction approaches, synthetic and field data examples demonstrate that the proposed I-TWC method achieves higher reconstruction accuracy. In addition, the reconstructed seismic traces exhibit improved continuity and curvature preservation, indicating their effectiveness for complex multidimensional seismic data recovery.
