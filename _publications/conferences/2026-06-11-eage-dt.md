**Title:** Simultaneous reconstruction and denoising via a self-supervised low-rank framework DeepTensor

**Authors:** Chengxiong Ding, Rongzhi Lin and Siwei Yu

**Conference:** 87th EAGE Annual Conference & Exhibition

**Date:** June 11, 2026

**Abstract:** Seismic data are often affected by missing traces and noise. While conventional low-rank approximation methods often result in over-smoothing or artifacts when facing non-Gaussian and coherent noise, supervised deep learning based methods are constrained by the scarcity of clean training labels and limited generalization capabilities. To handle this challenges, we propose a self-supervised low-rank framework, termed DeepTensor, for the simultaneous reconstruction and denoising of seismic data. Inspired by deep image priors, we use deep neural networks to parameterize the factors of a low-rank decomposition in a zero-shot manner, thereby integrating the implicit regularization of network architectures with the intrinsic low-rank structure of multidimensional seismic data. Numerical examples demonstrate that the proposed DeepTensor method achieves effective reconstruction and denoising performance and can be extended to higher dimensions seismic process.
